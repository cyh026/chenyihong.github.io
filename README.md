# Yihong Chen
Ph.D., Professor

Reserve Candidate for Academic and Technical Leaders of Sichuan Province, Distinguished Member of the China Computer Federation (CCF), Member of the Intelligent Systems Engineering Committee of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), and Member of the Academic Degree Evaluation Committee (Discipline Review Group) of the Sichuan Provincial People's Government.

**Research Interests**: Pattern Recognition, Computer Vision, Passive Behavior Sensing
